# How to install and try NanoGone

**Plain summary**
1. On your phone, open: https://github.com/McDc777/NanoGone/releases/tag/debug-latest (log in to GitHub if asked).
2. Tap **NanoGone-debug.apk** to download it (about 470 MB, because all the AI brains are inside; use Wi-Fi).
3. Open the downloaded file. If the phone asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app.
4. Open NanoGone once with Wi-Fi on: on Galaxy Ultras it fetches the AI chip's helper library one time. After that it works fully offline.
5. Open any photo in Samsung Gallery or Google Photos, tap **Share**, pick **NanoGone**. Or open NanoGone and tap **Choose a photo**.
6. Copies are saved in the **NanoGone** album. Your original is never changed.
7. On a Galaxy Ultra, tap **Add the deep brain** on the welcome screen once, on Wi-Fi (about 5 GB). After that, big removals get a second, deeper pass that also cleans shadows and reflections.

## Quick tour

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Tap** | Tap a thing: the AI wraps its edge in morning mist. Tap a misty piece again to unpick it. Zoom in first for tiny things. |
| **Brush** | Paint over wires, hair, scratches. The brush gets finer as you zoom in. |
| **Loop** | Draw a loop around a thing. |
| **Spot** | One tap on dust or a speck. |
| **Unpick** | Paint over mist to take it away. |
| **Find** | Find distractions (background people, bins, cars, clutter) or Find text (writing, date stamps, watermarks). |
| **Enhance** | Light and colour, Sharper and cleaner, Bigger 2x or 4x, Face fix, with a strength slider. |
| **Remove** | The mist lifts and the thing is gone. Shadows go too while "Shadows: on". |
| **Eye** (top bar) | Hold it to see the original. |
| **Save** | Top-quality JPEG or Lossless PNG, each with its size shown. |

## What to check and tell me

1. Does a removal look natural on your photos (people, bins, wires, specks)?
2. How long does a removal take on each device? (The welcome screen shows which chip the AI uses: NPU, GPU or CPU.)
3. Is the saved copy the same size as the original, with no stamp?
4. Anything that felt slow, confusing or ugly.

## What happens after a big removal

The fast brain shows the result at once. If the thing was big (or had a shadow) and the deep brain is added, a line says "The deep brain is giving it a second, finer pass". A little later the result quietly gets better. Undo still takes the whole removal back.

## HDR photos

Galaxy photos that glow brighter on HDR screens (Ultra HDR) keep that glow in the copy. Where you removed something, the glow is repaired too, so the removed thing does not shine through.

## Known gaps in this version

- Bigger is limited to results up to 64 MP for now.
- The deep brain needs a phone with 8 GB of memory or more (all the Galaxy Ultras). On the Tab it is hidden for now.
- Very large PNG saves (200 MP) may run out of memory.
