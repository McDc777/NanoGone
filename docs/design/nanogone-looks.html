Look A

The Invisible Mender

Like a tailor who mends a moth hole in a tweed coat with threads taken from the hem, so the hole is simply gone.

Colours
Charcoal tweed, linen white, and one saffron chalk colour for what you select.
Letters
Bodoni Moda for titles, like a fashion house. Hanken Grotesk for small, clear text.
Signature
Tap Remove. Threads weave across the hole in the colours around it, then melt into the photo.
Feels
Calm, crafted, precise. Dark, so photo colours look true.

Look B

The Conservator's Lamp

Like a museum expert repairing an old painting late at night, with a UV lamp that shows exactly where the repair hides.

Colours
Deep umber studio, gesso white, and a violet UV glow.
Letters
Cormorant Garamond for titles, like museum labels. Jost for small, clear text.
Signature
After a removal, press and drag on the photo. A UV lamp shows what was there, only under your finger. Lift your finger and it is gone again.
Feels
Expert, quiet, trustworthy. Dark, so photo colours look true.

Look C

Fresh Powder

Like fresh snow overnight on a mountain, hiding yesterday's ski tracks so the slope looks brand new.

Colours
Bright snow, deep mountain blue, and one piste-marker orange.
Letters
Bricolage Grotesque for bold, friendly titles. Figtree for small, clear text.
Signature
Tap Remove. Snow drifts into the spot, then melts away to show the clean photo.
Feels
Bright, friendly, light. A light screen instead of a dark one.

My pick

B, The Conservator's Lamp. Its trick is the thing you will do most: checking your work. The dark studio also keeps photo colours true, the way pro photo editors do.

This page shows the editing screen only. The look you choose also gets its own app icon, a light and a dark version, and its own animations on every screen.
