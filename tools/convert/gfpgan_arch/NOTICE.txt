gfpganv1_clean_arch.py and stylegan2_clean_arch.py come from https://github.com/TencentARC/GFPGAN
(Apache License 2.0). Changes for NanoGone: basicsr imports replaced by small stand-ins, and the
modulated convolution rewritten into an equivalent fixed-weight form (input scaled by the style,
demodulation applied to the output) so it runs on phone graphics chips. Checked equal to the
original to 3e-6.
